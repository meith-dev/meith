# Member guide

This guide is for people using an existing Meith board. You do not need to
install Meith. Open your community's board in a browser; its administrators
decide which forums and features are available to you.

## Join and sign in

Choose **Register** and complete the form. The board may ask you to confirm
your email address, wait for administrator approval, or do both before you
can sign in. Follow the message shown after registration.

If there is no registration link, the board may have closed registrations.
Contact its organisers. Use the password-reset link on the sign-in page if
you already have an account but cannot remember the password.

Some boards also offer a sign-in provider or a passkey. If you have enabled
two-factor authentication, keep your recovery codes somewhere you can reach
without the device that holds your authenticator app.

## Find a discussion

Open a forum to see its threads, then open a thread to read the posts and
replies. The board's navigation may include **New posts**, **Unanswered**, or
**My posts**. Administrators can rename or hide these links.

**Search** lets you enter words and narrow the results by forum, author,
period, and other filters. You can choose to search titles or whole posts
and to group results by thread or post. Search only returns content you
have permission to see. If the board asks you to wait before searching
again, wait for the stated interval.

If you cannot see a forum or a posting button, your account may need
activation or a group permission. A forum can also be closed to new posts.
Ask the organisers if the access looks wrong.

## Start a thread or reply

1. Open the appropriate forum and choose **New thread**.
2. Enter a clear title and write the post.
3. Add attachments or a poll if the forum offers them.
4. Submit the post. If approval is required, wait for a moderator to review it.

To join an existing discussion, use its reply composer. Use **Quote** when
you need to show which part of another post you are answering.

The [formatting guide](./formatting.md) explains Markdown, links, code,
spoilers, mentions, and attachments. You can type the syntax with JavaScript
disabled; toolbar buttons and live previews need JavaScript.

Editing and deleting your own posts depend on the board's permissions and
edit-time limit. Saved drafts are available under `/usercp/drafts`.

## Follow threads and manage notifications

Use the thread's subscription control to follow replies. Open
`/notifications` to read notifications and
`/notifications/preferences` to choose how the board contacts you.

Email notifications and board announcements are optional. The preferences
page also offers an activity digest on boards that support it. Delivery
requires the board's mail service to be configured. Web push, when enabled
by the board, also needs your browser's permission.

Under `/usercp/options`, choose whether threads you participate in are
followed automatically.

## Send a private message

Open **Messages** at `/messages`, then compose a message to the intended
member. Conversations list replies together. Messaging can be disabled by
the board, limited by group permissions, or restricted by the recipient's
contact preferences.

## Manage your account

Open your account panel at `/usercp`. On a small screen, use its menu to
switch sections.

| Page | What you can change |
|---|---|
| `/usercp/profile` | Profile details |
| `/usercp/avatar` | Your avatar |
| `/usercp/signature` | The signature shown with your posts, when allowed |
| `/usercp/options` | Language, timezone, paging, and automatic thread subscriptions |
| `/usercp/security` | Account security, sessions, and available sign-in methods |
| `/usercp/contacts` | Buddy and ignore lists |

The appearance control lets you choose among the themes enabled by the
board. Available options vary with the board's configuration.

## Ask for help or report a problem

Use **Report** on a post that needs a moderator's attention and explain the
problem. Reports go to the moderation team. For account access or board
settings, contact the organisers through the contact details they publish.

If the board offers **Thanks** or **Rate**, you can use it to recognise a
helpful contribution. The board may require a minimum post count or a
comment before you can give a rating.
