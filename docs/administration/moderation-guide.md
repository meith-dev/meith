# Moderate discussions

Moderators approve content, handle reports and manage conversations within their assigned forums. Open `/modcp` and check **My forums** (`/modcp/forums`) to see the actions you are allowed to perform.

## Find the work

| Page | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `/moderation` | Threads and posts awaiting approval |
| `/moderation/reports` | Reports from members |
| `/modcp/forums` | Your appointments and allowed actions |
| `/modcp/log` | Recorded moderation actions |
| `/modcp/ip` | Address lookup, restricted to administrators and super-moderators |

Thread pages also have **Moderator tools**. Forum and thread lists offer selection controls for bulk actions. The available controls reflect your permissions; access to the moderator panel does not itself grant every action.

## Approve or reject held content

Open the approval queue, read the content and select the intended items. Use **Approve selected** or **Reject selected**, then check the queue and the affected discussion.

Content can be held because of new-member spam controls, a forum/group approval rule or a warning restriction. A moderate-posting warning applies even when the author would otherwise bypass approval as staff.

## Handle a report

Open a report, review the reported item and claim or handle it according to the available controls. Record the outcome and close the report when the work is complete. The reporter can be notified of the outcome.

Reports can concern posts, threads, profiles or private messages. A private-message report makes that reported item available for staff review; it does not expose everybody's inbox.

Community flagging may hide content after the configured threshold. Review the actual content before deciding to restore or remove it; a threshold is a signal for review, not proof of misconduct.

## Manage a discussion

Choose the smallest action that resolves the problem: edit, lock, pin, move, split, merge, delete or restore. Permissions are separate for each operation and can differ between source and destination forums.

Before a move or merge, check the destination's audience. Moving a thread can change who may read it. A merge selects a surviving thread; a split creates a separate discussion from selected posts. Read the form's target and confirmation before submitting.

Deletion and restoration are different grants. Do not assume that permission to remove a post also permits restoring it.

## Use warnings and bans

Warnings add points and can expire or be revoked. Configured warning levels may restrict posting, hold content for approval or ban the member. Read the selected warning's effect before applying it and explain the reason clearly.

Warning points are not percentages. Revoking points does not automatically undo every ban already imposed by a level; check the member's current restrictions. Ban expiry depends on scheduled work.

Board-wide member management and ban settings may require an administrator. Escalate when the necessary action is outside your appointment.

## Review the result

Check the affected discussion and the moderator log. Confirm that content has the intended visibility and that another moderator can understand what happened. For an unexpected access denial, ask an administrator to inspect [forum permissions and appointments](forums.md).
