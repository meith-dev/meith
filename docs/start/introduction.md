## Requirements

A deployed board needs PostgreSQL, persistent upload storage, email delivery and a worker or scheduler. Its repository selects the Meith version, themes and plugins.

Administrators manage settings and members. Moderators manage content in assigned forums. Operators maintain hosting, backups and upgrades. One person can perform several roles.

Themes control appearance; plugins add features. Most reading and posting works without JavaScript.
