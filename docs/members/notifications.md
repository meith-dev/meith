# Follow discussions and manage notifications

Choose which discussions to follow and how to receive updates. You need a signed-in account; email and browser delivery also depend on services enabled by the community.

## Follow or stop following a thread

Use a thread's subscription control to follow replies. Manage followed discussions at `/subscriptions`. At `/usercp/options`, choose whether threads you participate in should be followed automatically.

Muting and unfollowing change what updates you receive; neither deletes your posts. An email unsubscribe action does not delete the thread.

## Read your notifications

Open `/notifications` to see activity directed to you. A repeated event may be grouped with an existing unread notification. Opening the notification follows its link to the relevant discussion or activity.

On-site notifications remain available even when you turn email delivery off.

## Choose email and digest preferences

Open `/notifications/preferences` and review the available event types, subscriptions and digest choices. Save the settings and verify the next expected update.

Instant delivery still depends on the board's scheduled work. If a whole community is waiting for mail, ask an organiser to check the scheduler and mail service. Password-reset delivery uses its own request path and should be diagnosed separately.

## Enable browser notifications

If the board offers web push, opt in through its controls and grant permission in your browser. Browser permission alone is not a board subscription. You can change the browser permission later through its site settings.

For operator setup and device limitations, see [Web push](../operations/web-push.md).

## Stop unwanted updates

Review both thread subscriptions and notification preferences. Use the unsubscribe action in a legitimate board email where appropriate. Ask organisers about community mail you cannot identify rather than sharing a private unsubscribe link publicly.
