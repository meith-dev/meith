# Messages and contacts

Sign in and open `/messages`.

## Send a message

1. Open the compose page.
2. Select recipients and enter a subject and message.
3. Check the recipients, then send.
4. Open the conversation to confirm delivery to your sent view.

**Reply** addresses the author. Check recipients before including other people. See [Formatting](formatting.md) for message syntax.

## Limits

Group permissions, recipient preferences, daily send allowances and storage quotas can block delivery. One send counts once toward the daily allowance, regardless of recipient count. Deleting messages frees storage but does not reset that allowance. A full recipient inbox can prevent sending.

## Contacts and reports

Manage buddies and ignored members at `/usercp/contacts`. Ignoring hides post bodies where supported; it does not delete posts or shared history.

Use **Report** on a message requiring staff review. Staff can review the reported message; reporting does not grant general access to inboxes.
