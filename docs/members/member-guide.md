# Use your community

Open your community's Meith board in a browser. You do not need to install software to participate. Available forums and features depend on the community's settings and your permissions.

## Join and sign in

Choose **Register**, complete the form and follow the confirmation or approval message. If registration is closed, contact the organisers. Use the password-reset link if you already have an account.

Keep recovery codes when enabling two-factor authentication. [Account security](account-security.md) explains passwords, passkeys, sign-in providers and devices.

## Find a discussion

Open a forum and choose a thread. **New posts**, **Unanswered** and **My posts** help find activity; organisers may customize navigation labels.

Use **Search** to look for words, then narrow by forum, author, time or the other offered filters. Choose titles or whole posts and thread or post results as appropriate. You can only find content you are permitted to read.

If a forum or posting control is missing, check account activation and ask the organisers about access. A closed forum may allow reading but not new posts.

## Start a thread or reply

1. Choose the forum and **New thread**.
2. Enter a descriptive title and write the message.
3. Add allowed attachments or a poll if needed.
4. Review and submit. Content held for approval appears after moderator review.

Reply in an existing thread to continue that discussion. Use **Quote** to show which post you are answering. [Write and format posts](formatting.md) covers Markdown and attachments. Saved drafts are under `/usercp/drafts`.

Editing and deletion depend on permissions and any time limit. Contact a moderator when a correction is outside the controls available to you.

## Stay in touch

| Task | Guide or page |
|---|---|
| Follow a thread or change email preferences | [Notifications](notifications.md) |
| Send a private message or manage contacts | [Messages](messages.md) |
| Change profile details, avatar or signature | Account panel at `/usercp` |
| Set language, timezone or automatic following | `/usercp/options` |
| Review sign-in methods and devices | [Account security](account-security.md) |
| Respond to a community event | [Calendar](../administration/calendar.md) |

Optional features such as awards and paid memberships appear only when the community enables them.

## Ask for help or report a problem

Use **Report** on content that needs moderator attention and explain the issue. For account access or community policy, use the organisers' published contact route.

A Thanks or rating control recognizes a contribution. Whether it appears depends on the community's reputation settings and your eligibility.

## Membership payments and receipts

If your community offers paid membership, open **Membership** to view its plans. Use **Your membership** to manage access and renewals. **Recent receipts → View receipt** opens a receipt for an eligible paid purchase you made, including a gift you bought. Free purchases and payments awaiting confirmation have no receipt link. Subscription renewal invoices are in the billing portal.

Contact the community's administrators if a payment or membership needs investigation. The [Dues guide](../administration/membership-guide.md) describes the available plans and administration tools.
