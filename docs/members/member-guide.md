# Read and post

Open the community's board in a browser. Available forums and actions depend on your account permissions.

## Join

Select **Register**, complete the form and follow the activation instructions. If registration is closed, contact the administrators. For an existing account, use the sign-in or password-reset link.

## Find discussions

Open a forum or use **New posts**, **Unanswered**, **My posts** or **Search**. Search filters can narrow results by forum, author, time and content type. Results include only content you may read.

## Post

1. Open a forum and select **New thread**, or open a thread to reply.
2. Enter the title and message. Use **Quote** to include an earlier post.
3. Add permitted attachments or a poll.
4. Submit. Posts held for approval appear after moderator review.

See [Formatting](formatting.md) for Markdown. Resume saved drafts at `/usercp/drafts`. Editing and deletion may have permission or time limits.

## Account and activity

| Task | Page |
|---|---|
| Profile, avatar and signature | `/usercp` |
| Language, timezone and automatic following | `/usercp/options` |
| Follow discussions | [Notifications](notifications.md) |
| Private messages and contacts | [Messages](messages.md) |
| Passwords, passkeys and devices | [Account security](account-security.md) |

Use **Report** on content requiring moderator review. Contact administrators for account or access problems.

## Paid membership

If Dues is enabled, open **Membership** to buy a plan and **Your membership** to manage it. **Recent receipts → View receipt** opens eligible paid-purchase receipts, including gifts you bought. Free or unconfirmed purchases have no receipt link. Subscription renewal invoices are in the billing portal.
